Frequently Asked Questions

Is this logo maker free?

Yes. You can create and export logos using this browser-based tool without paying for a subscription.

Can I download a transparent logo?

Yes. Enable the transparent background option and export your logo as a PNG or SVG.

What is an SVG logo?

SVG is a vector image format. It can scale to different sizes without the pixelation associated with ordinary raster images.

What is a favicon?

A favicon is a small icon associated with a website. It commonly appears in browser tabs and bookmarks.

Are my designs uploaded to a server?

No. Logo generation and image exports happen in your browser. This page does not upload your logo design to a server.
