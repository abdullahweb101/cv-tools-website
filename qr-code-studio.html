Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create an Instagram or Facebook QR code?

Yes. Enter your profile URL or username and generate a QR code that opens your profile.

Does the Raast QR support bank transfers?

The Raast P2P option builds the P2P payload according to the published SBP format. Always test it with a participating bank app and verify the beneficiary before confirming a transfer.

Does the Easypaisa QR directly receive payments?

The Easypaisa number option shares your mobile/account number in a QR code. It is not a substitute for an official Easypaisa-generated payment QR.

Can I make a bank account QR?

Yes. You can encode bank name, account title, account number and IBAN as shareable account details. For a payment-standard QR, use the Raast P2P option.

Are these dynamic QR codes?

No. These are static QR codes. Their encoded information cannot be edited after creation.

Is QR Code Studio free?

Yes. You can generate and download QR codes without a paid subscription.
