Frequently Asked Questions

Is QR Code Studio free?

Yes. You can generate and download QR codes without a paid subscription.

Can I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Enter your Wi-Fi network name, password and security type. A compatible phone can scan the code to connect to the network.

Can I add my company logo?

Yes. Upload a logo to place it in the center. Use high error correction and test the QR code on multiple devices before printing.

What is the difference between PNG and SVG?

PNG is suitable for normal digital use. SVG is a vector format that can scale to larger sizes without pixelation.

Are these dynamic QR codes?

No. These are static QR codes. Their encoded information cannot be edited after creation. To change a destination, generate a new QR code.
