About CV Tools

Welcome to CV Tools, an online platform designed to make everyday document tasks easier. Our goal is to provide simple and accessible tools that help people create professional resumes and work with PDF documents through their web browsers.

What We Offer

  • CV Maker: Create a professional CV using different layouts and download it as a PDF.
  • PDF Scanner: Turn uploaded images or camera captures into PDF documents.
  • PDF Tools: Merge PDF files, extract pages, rotate pages and organize documents.

Our Approach

We focus on straightforward tools, simple interfaces and practical features. We aim to make common document tasks easier without requiring users to install desktop software.

Privacy and Ease of Use

Our current tools are designed to process documents directly in your browser. The CV Maker also uses your browser's local storage to save CV information on your device. Please read our Privacy Policy for more information about browser storage and document processing.

Our Goal

Our goal is to keep improving CV Tools by developing useful, accessible and easy-to-use online document utilities.
