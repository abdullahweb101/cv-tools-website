Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our online tools.

Are the tools free to use?

Yes. The tools available on this website are currently provided free of charge.

Can I create a CV online?

Yes. Use our CV Maker to enter your personal information, add your experience and education, choose a template and download your CV as a PDF.

Can I create a personal portfolio online?

Yes. Our Portfolio Maker lets you build a personal portfolio using 15 templates. You can add your skills, experience, education and projects, then export it as HTML or PDF.

Can I convert photos into a PDF?

Yes. Our PDF Scanner lets you upload images or capture documents with your camera and create a PDF file.

What can I do with the PDF Tools?

You can merge PDF files, extract selected pages, split documents, rotate pages and organize PDF files.

Do I need to install any software?

No. You can access the tools directly through a supported web browser without installing a desktop application.
