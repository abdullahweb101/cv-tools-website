Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our free online tools.

Are the tools free to use?

The tools on this website are currently provided free of charge. Some features may depend on browser compatibility or third-party libraries used by an individual tool.

Can I create a professional CV online?

Yes. Use our CV Maker to add your personal information, work experience, education and skills. Choose a layout and download your CV as a PDF.

Can I build a personal portfolio?

Yes. Our Portfolio Maker helps you showcase your profile, projects, services, skills and professional links. You can export your portfolio in the available formats.

What can I do with Image Studio?

Image Studio provides image resizing, format conversion, quality adjustment and batch image processing features.

Can I generate QR codes for different purposes?

Yes. QR Code Studio supports different content types, including website links, text, Wi-Fi details, WhatsApp, email, phone numbers and contact information.

Can I create a logo for my business?

Yes. Logo Studio Pro lets you customize logo designs with text, shapes, symbols, colours and uploaded graphics. You can export your finished logo in supported formats.

What is Brand Identity Studio used for?

Brand Identity Studio helps you explore colour palettes, gradients, contrast and font combinations for a consistent visual identity.

Can I combine multiple PDF files?

Yes. Use Merge PDF Files to combine multiple documents into one PDF. You can also use Split PDF Files to extract or separate selected pages.

Can I scan photos and save them as PDF?

Yes. PDF Scanner lets you upload images or capture documents with a compatible camera and create PDF files.

Do I need to install software?

No desktop installation is required for the browser-based tools. Open the relevant tool page using a supported web browser.
