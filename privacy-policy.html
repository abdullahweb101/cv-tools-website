Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to CV Tools. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website and use our online tools.

1. Information You Provide

When using our tools, you may enter personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, work experience, education and professional links. You may also select photographs and PDF documents for processing.

2. Browser Storage

Our CV Maker uses your browser's local storage to save CV information so that you can return to your work later using the same browser and device.

This information may remain stored on your device until you clear it through the CV Maker or remove the relevant browser data. If you use a shared device, avoid saving sensitive personal information in the browser.

3. Document Processing

Our CV Maker, PDF Scanner and PDF Tools are designed to process the documents you select directly in your browser. The current tools do not require you to create an account or upload your document files to our own document-processing server.

Some features use third-party JavaScript libraries delivered through external content delivery networks. Your browser may contact those providers to load the libraries required by the tools.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use browser storage or similar technologies required for its features. Third-party services, if added to the website in the future, may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Website Hosting and Technical Information

Our website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Like other website hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, security information and request logs as part of providing and protecting the service. Please review the hosting provider's privacy information for further details.

6. Third-Party Services

Our tools may use third-party software libraries and services to provide certain features. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the relevant policies when appropriate.

7. Data Security

We aim to provide tools that process documents in the browser. However, no website, browser or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid entering information that you do not wish to store or process on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Anyone using the website should do so with appropriate guidance where required.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools or practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our website, please visit our Contact Us page.
